### What must hold this table together

Paragraph **3 establishes the earlier conflict**; paragraph **4 develops its twentieth-century continuation and surrounding currents**; paragraph **5 explains where constructive work continued and why immense progress does not, in our argument, vindicate the prevailing philosophical direction**.

The refuge paragraph belongs here because it explains the same historical situation. It is not a separate movement inserted as a concession after the indictment.

**Current manuscript adjustment:** the existing paragraph beginning “The loss of our grip gathered momentum…” already includes twentieth-century doctrines. That material must be redistributed when we write paragraphs 3 and 4, rather than repeated. The isolated “As if this wasn't enough,” remains an unfinished transition; its wording is not settled.

**Material for paragraph 4:** the [philosophical target dossier](<../Work/Epistemic-Dogmas/EPISTEMIC-DOGMAS-INDIVIDUAL-FAMILIAL-AND-SYSTEMIC-INDICTMENT.md>) supplies candidate arguments around verificationism, operationalism, confirmationism, epistemic probabilism, explanatory reductionism, linguistic or framework confinement, dialectical immunity and instrumentalism. We will select the combination that makes this paragraph work. These are possible contents of **one paragraph**, not additional paragraphs or compulsory items.

**Possible closing connection in paragraph 5:** the manuscript's note about constructive epistemic capabilities sheltered in computing meeting instrumentalist aspirations may explain the conjunction that produced the “ultimate instrument.” Its precise formulation is still to develop.

**Passage into Group 3:** the historical account has prepared both the capacities that made the engine possible and the philosophical expectations that greet it. We now turn to the instrument itself.

The manuscript's older loose notes suggest one order; we will settle it through the present discussion rather than impose one now.

## After these groups — Outlining our position

This is the destination prepared by Group 3, **not yet another table of invented paragraphs**.

The argument will move toward the Formal Industrial Revolution as a civilizational prospect: formal and causal constructions at the center of new industrial capacities; epistemic techne and technical embodiment developing together; the probabilistic engine as a major enabling power rather than a substitute for that construction.

The three companion works deepen the philosophical responsibilities, domain modelling and constructive embodiment. Here we must establish the overarching position and its consequences. We have not yet settled this next group's paragraph structure or where the introduction gives way to the body.

## Notes retained for placement during development

- **An epistemic dark age with substantial powers:** the contrast between technical potency and the diagnosed intellectual condition; possible placement near the historical conclusion or the engine's risk.
- **Deification, mysticism and fear surrounding the engine:** possible symptoms within that diagnosis, to develop through their precise argumentative relevance.
- **“God knows for how long” / “God has entered the discussion again”:** a possible rhetorical return from the existing opening; wording and usefulness remain open.

- **The enabling range:** new machines, domain modelling and new or newly operable techne. This material belongs to the constructive opening and subsequent positive account, with its depth and placement to be developed.
