## Final consequential revisions

- Economism now attacks the unsupported fixed sequence and exclusive one-way dependence; it does not infer causal independence merely from the efficacy of ideas.
- The root map no longer confuses psychological or economic explanatory reduction with genetic dismissal of an argument.
- The prophecy-to-conspiracy mechanism explicitly identifies the extra premise that attributes deviations to deliberate obstruction.
- The obscurity/totality compound does not convert a conditionally formulated strong-totality doctrine into a directly documented Adorno position.
- Direct spontaneous generation and the origin of adaptive organization are distinguished from the related gradualist omission.
- Simulation counting and doomsday reasoning receive explicit treatment without claiming a complete refutation of Bostrom or adopting Deutsch's confident immortality forecast.
- Collective decisions under declared rules are distinguished from revelation of a naturally given, rule-independent collective mind.
- Designer-supplied knowledge and later knowledge creation are allowed to coexist; the question concerns the capacity demonstrated by the result.
- General dismissal of explanatory adequacy is distinguished from withholding commitment to an unsettled particular interpretation.
- The europium example now identifies architectural substitution, not merely the hope of finding more resources. Absolute precaution is also tested against its own introduction.
- The final chapter explains how a false epistemology can appropriate genuine achievements, without treating success as vindication or falsity as proof of uniformly destructive historical consequences.
