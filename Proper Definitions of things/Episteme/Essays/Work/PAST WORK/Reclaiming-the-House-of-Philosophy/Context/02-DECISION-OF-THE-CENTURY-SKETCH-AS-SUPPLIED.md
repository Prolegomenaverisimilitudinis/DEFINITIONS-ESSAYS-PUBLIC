## 2.1 **The decision of the Century **   


We are now entering an hour of need and a critical inflection point which will determine where we will end up at the beginning of the 22nd Century, and the time to decide and to mobilize systemically is now, and it could be argued that we are too late. One might suspect that I am talking about the "Intellgicence" inflection point again, but I am not. I am talking about the philosophical position which we take in relation to our own future. Are we going to take the future in our own hands with a determinist, realist and epistemically optimist attitude and action, or are we going to externalize it to the most radical version of instrumentalism thinkable? That instrumentalism isn't just in externalizing our thinking to an probabilistic instrument, that is a mere symptom of multiple cronical conditions with which we are inflicted for more than a century, and even the instrumentalist disease is merely a derrived problem which has other pre-conditioned disiese, from positivism which can validate an occurence and refusing to talk about the underlying structures, to the lingusitic "turns" - as Wittgenstein would say - we performed as intellectual evasions our problems. 

So yes, the problem is more foundational than the mere capabiltiies of the Probabilistic Engine, which can now operate over our symbolic worlds in a programmable fashion, and relative to the difficulty of the function made to perform, is quite stable. For pragmatic purposes as well as argumentative ones, we can say that if one programms an instruction as simple as;"perform arithmetic outputs and only give the final answer", then any single time when we place 2+2 as input, its output will be 4. In that bounded sense, if all conditions are met, and abstracted away, we can say that its output can be trivially causal under the correct conditions.

 The temptation is to make the Engine the core exists precisely because the rational worlds described in Part I have never been independently constituted so they can be imprinted into such logical system, nor do such systems exist. So the question is, what are we going to do? Are we going to give it huge instructions and make it the core in world-scale distributed systems, or are we going to build the rational structures and reduce it to a kernel daemon? This is a question not about the technology, is a question about what we want for the future, of who we want to be in the future, and of what type of successors we are to be to our Enghlintened forefathers.

Are we, at this critical point, going to keep hiding together with the legacy of the Ionian tradition under rocks and leave all these philosophical disiease permiate into the future and be lesser successors of our Enghlintened forefathers, or are we going to get out of hiding and start building? And Europe, where are you? Are you not ashamed of yourself? Have you not self-flagelated enough by staying hidden in basements, in cafes and in your laboratories while seeing the decline and allowing relativism to permiate around you? Where is the powerhouse of reason at this critical juncture? 

We should have learned by now, that what we know (in the fallible sense) is not enough! Understanding our capabilities and being able to operate around them is not enough, and we are definitely not doing enough. And I am speaking with you, European man, or should I say Ionian man? With all of you who are the inheritors of the Ionian tradition, no matter where you have been born or grew up, those friends of knowledge momentarily finding themselves at risk of being lesser successors of their intellectual ancestors. While this addresses all of our brothers and sisters of the Ionian tradition, it is indeed especially addresed to Europe and the USA, which are the powerhouses of the tradition and its main inheritors in both similar as well as in distinct ways. On these grounds however, they have collectively failed us both, in their own ways. 

What should be clear by now, is that all of those who can read between the lines have failed to fight for our civilizations best philosophical approaches in many areas on the battlefield of ideas mainly because they thought distribution was beneath them, or they thought the best argument should reproduce memetically.This is especially true for the recent past, and is still true for us today. And look where that led to, look where we stand today; We stand in a place where the question posed with a straight face is not IF we are going to outsource a lot of our future to Instrumentalism and positivism or inductivism - and god forbit, to relativism - but how the ride itsellf will be. WIll it be smooth? Will it be bumpy? How will it feel? This is the philosophical state in which we find ourselves.

And even worse, is not that the tradition itself at its core is weeker, if anything, it is more powerful than ever techne wise, as well as its hidden communities more potent than ever. On the techne side, the 20th century has seen incredible advancements in mathematic and logic in all of its forms, especially in integrative parts. That is visible across our civilization, their integrations across physics, chemestry, etc- and especially in computing or engineering, it is silently permiating across our critical infrastructure and have verticalized fully and made operable across the stack. In the broad sense however, outside of pockets of the core, it is hidden, it is almost taboo to introduce proper riguor into the conversation, and the conversation itself, my friends, will decide where we will end up in the 22nd Century, it will be the one which will allocate resources and make best for the nature of our civilziation forward. 

The time to decide where our responsibilities lie between our predecessors, ourselves, and our successors, has to be made now. And now, in this hour, even if most of the practicioners and contributors to the Ionian tradition decide today to step up, make no mistake; the time for debate or for a battle of ideas is at two decades late for that fun party. Beside that, I think its safe to conclude that we were incapable of pursuing that route anyway for different constitutive reasons.

Our only chance now, is to build, and to build feverishly while at it, because we will have to sidestep most of the current logical technological substrates, and to rethink things from scratch in order to develop the right techne which will give us vertical stack capability integration and instantiation in technological substrates, and for the love of everything good, we nend to place ourselves primarily in the fallible position, and hence responsible for any conjecture, and hypothesis, any techne or system design and their collision with reality. That is what is required of us, not to externalize our responsability to any instrumentalist means, neither to go toward any instrumentalist end, be it a once-upon-a-time methodology, or a language model or any similar future instrument.
