# Author clarification — a cleared but abandoned house

Received during the source-reading and revision cycle of 10 October 2026.

The author sharpened the role initially expressed through “Popper the Gardener”: Popper's importance is not merely tidying a garden. The decisive image is that the house has been cleared of its disorder, but remains abandoned. Even the former disorderly occupants have gone; their absence has not supplied the people or constructive means for the next chapter.

## Consequence for the definition

The historical/critical movement must not leave the reader expecting endless repetition of the old demolition. It must arrive at the difference between removing an obstruction and undertaking what that removal makes possible.

The current MD incorporates this in §6.7 and carries it into §§7.3, 9, 12, 14 and 18. The philosophical image is distinguished from a historical assertion that every philosophical error disappeared, every objection was conclusively settled, or no constructive philosopher remains anywhere.

The positive demand is to inhabit the house through actual work: creation, adaptation and integration of the epistemic means and arrangements that the next problems require. Existing clearances and constructions are beginnings and resources for that work, not substitutes for it.
