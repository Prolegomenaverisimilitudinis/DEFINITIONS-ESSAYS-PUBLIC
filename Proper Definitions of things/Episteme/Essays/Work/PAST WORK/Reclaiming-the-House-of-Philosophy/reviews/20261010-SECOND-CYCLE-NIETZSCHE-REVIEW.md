## Prioritized corrections

### P2 — The decisive tavern scene briefly reads as completed history

**Canvas §6.10, line 323:** the paragraph beginning “The decisive image is no longer a hall filled with disorderly revelers” says the gathering has dispersed and the vows of omnipotence have exhausted themselves. It narrates a completed collective condition before line 325 identifies the historical claim as proposed and unestablished. It also sits awkwardly beside §6.8, line 307, which correctly allows the old doctrines to keep circulating after their inadequacies have been exposed.

**Minimum correction:** make the first sentence explicitly establish a scene within the analogy: “In the decisive scene of our analogy, the gathering has dispersed.” Keep the subsequent description of exhausted vows and closed doors. That preserves the user's wanted abandoned-house turn without asserting that actual historical schools collectively disappeared. Alternatively: “The decisive image concerns what remains even where the gathering has dispersed.” No extra historical disclaimer is needed after the paragraph; the adjacent qualification already exists.

### P2 — Removing and constructing must not be made mutually exclusive

**Canvas §6.10, line 335:** “An account removed is not a successor account created” follows an appeal to Nietzsche's §12. The intended distinction is sound, but this sentence can read as categorical separation. Nietzsche's actual paragraph, reading-copy line 411, both rejects the indivisible soul and proposes successor hypotheses involving mortality, multiplicity and a social structure of drives. His clearing already performs some constructive work. The canvas itself likewise recognizes constructive clearing through Popper and Tarski at lines 305 and 313.

**Minimum correction:** “Removing an account does not by itself complete an adequate successor account.” This is the exact distinction the argument needs: criticism can include construction without supplying all subsequent constructive responsibilities. The rest of the paragraph can stay.

### P3 — Stale source count

**Canvas §5.1, line 160:** “biographies, institutional histories and comparisons that two philosophical books do not provide.” The current argument draws on Nietzsche, Deutsch, *Conjectures and Refutations* and *The Myth of the Framework*.

**Minimum correction:** replace “two philosophical books” with “these philosophical books.” The issue is the kind of evidence, not the count.

## One optional attribution refinement

**Canvas §6.2, line 214:** the document says it refuses the inference that explanation must therefore be domination. The strongest disagreement with Nietzsche is already stated, but “the inference” may imply that he derives that conclusion simply from the self-deception just diagnosed. His §9, reading-copy line 387, identifies philosophy itself with world-creating will to power; §211, line 1657, affirmatively identifies philosophical creation with law-giving. This is broader than a complaint about fraudulent explanation.

**Minimum refinement, if editing this sentence:** “Our account takes the diagnosis of self-deception seriously while rejecting his identification of philosophical creation with commanding will to power.” Retain the following sentence distinguishing concept creation from making every empirical claim true. This makes the disagreement more exact without multiplying qualifications.

## Required boundaries checked

No additional defect found in the Nietzsche use requiring a new general caveat. The following existing distinctions should survive the main agent's revision:

- **The strongest disagreement:** §6.2 line 214, §6.5 line 250 and §14 line 542 refuse Nietzsche's creative command/philosopher-legislator while adapting his diagnostic and preparatory distinctions. The source register, N1 and N3, lines 662–664, prevents the excluded premises being imported. Do not dilute this into merely saying the two authors sometimes disagree.
- **Active doubt versus abstention:** §6.5 line 250 retains the §§208–210 sequence instead of treating skepticism itself as the disease. Nietzsche's §210 makes critical discipline and experiment indispensable. The canvas's continuing criticism within construction preserves that meaning.
- **Scientist boundary:** §5.4 lines 186–190 explicitly locates the nineteenth-century charge in philosophical responsibility and recognizes scientific achievements. §6.7 line 278 scopes the later criticism to particular twentieth-century attitudes. No §206 attack on scientists, sex-based analogy or inherited incapacity has been imported.
- **Europe without inherited superiority:** §4.1 frames the Ionian inheritance as a practice; §14 lines 540–544 makes the call a demand rather than a title and includes participants elsewhere. N4 line 665 labels the national-material use as selective adaptation. Nietzsche's §256, reading-copy line 1971, supports a challenge to European national estrangement; it does not prove this document's Ionian genealogy or global openness. The current wording keeps those commitments ours.
- **Source disagreement beyond domination:** §18.3 line 673 records that Nietzsche's Hegel and Enlightenment judgments do not simply corroborate Popper. This must remain: §204 partly defends German historical culture against Schopenhauer's anti-Hegel effects, and the author's Preface treats democratic Enlightenment as relaxing spiritual tension.

## Explanatory status of the connected decline

The explanatory work is concentrated at §6.1 line 198, §6.2 lines 206–214, §6.3 lines 218–228 and §6.4 lines 232–242: the document names substitutions of standards, a mechanism for their transmission, and an invalid generalization from failed promise to failed vocation. The metaphors therefore have identifiable intellectual referents. That is a conditional mechanism account, not yet a substantiated causal history. Lines 198, 238, 274 and 325 already distinguish the two; no additional blanket warning would improve them.

The next requirement is actual historical or constructive cases if the canvas becomes a finished essay, rather than further denunciatory synonyms. Within this bounded revision, only the local historical overstatement at line 323 needs correction to keep that standing consistent.
