## Substantive revision cycles

1. **Recover and preserve.** Read the original methodology completely, recover the author's commission and separate the current challenged House draft from accepted direction. Preserve the previous methodology before expansion.
2. **Integrate the generative method.** Add issue and connective-premise discovery, definitions as supported instruments of judgment, differentiated causal work, coordinated narrative orders, reader participation, composition as intellectual development, tonal range, collective voice, and functional selection of particulars. Integrate these into existing sections rather than append a separate rhetoric manual.
3. **Complete the forward direction.** Distinguish the current analytical phase from the eventual whole definition. Require reclamation's responsibilities to arise from the diagnosed losses, surviving capacities and present demands, with their means and adequacy developed rather than supplied by exhortation.
4. **Return to sources and challenge the method.** Personally reread relevant argument, refutation, convergence, analogy, dissociation, compositional and Nietzsche passages. Assess supplementary findings. Integrate selective investigation of strong difficulties, distinguish independent grounds from repetition, and distinguish criticism's result from positive support for a replacement explanation.
5. **Correct conceptual drift.** Replace “resuming membership” with continuing membership and renewal of a common vocation; make contribution the basis of belonging. Remove the older “making the House habitable” phrasing in favour of the intellectual work performed by clearing. Preserve the author's proposed disciplinary distinctions without importing a textbook demarcation. Add the difference between a failure of quality within a vocation and substitution of another activity for it.
6. **Keep creative development open.** Clarify that an emerging conception, intuition or question need not already be a finished proposition. Composition and provisional distinctions can discover what the argument must become. This prevents the rhetoric books' classifications from becoming limits on the author's original philosophical development.

## Spot application to the existing challenged draft

The main assistant reread actual passages of the House draft, including its §§2–4, §6, §12 and the beginning of §14. These remain unedited and retain their challenged standing.

- **Family and vocation:** the draft already contains useful distinctions between belonging through work and institutional recognition. The expanded method preserves that possibility without treating all existing formulations as accepted. It also separates recognition of intellectual affinity from a specific claim of historical transmission.
- **Schism:** the draft passes between accounts attributed to Popper or Deutsch and broader causal claims about successors. The method directs the next development toward the particular passage from textual commitment to historical transmission and consequence, rather than treating attribution as closure of the causal explanation.
- **Computing:** the draft's §12 moves from affirmative constructive refuge to the limitations of operative systems. The expanded method makes the change of argumentative function visible and directs a writer to examine its placement against the author's intended emphasis. That is a concrete compositional issue, not a request for a generic paragraph explaining that compiling does not prove everything.
- **Forward turn:** the presence of responsibilities in the draft's §14 does not itself establish that the present diagnosis has been sufficiently developed. The method now distinguishes those achievements and requires the eventual responsibilities to have intelligible grounds and content. Existing proposed responsibilities remain material for assessment, not settled authorial decisions.

This exercise exposed no need to impose a new structure on the House definition. It clarified how to identify the actual next work while preserving the author's intended sequence and the status of the current text.
