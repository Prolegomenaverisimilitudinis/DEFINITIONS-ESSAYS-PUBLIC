## What this review does not establish

This is a coherent, source-informed first development, not a finished essay or an independently verified comprehensive history. In particular, the extent of the proposed late-eighteenth-century rupture, migration of talent, century of contraction and present abandonment still requires further evidence.

Popper and Deutsch support particular philosophical mechanisms and offer their own historical interpretations. Their testimony is not a license to state every broad claim in the author's frame as established history.

The next substantive collaboration should sharpen the content of the responsibility still left unfulfilled. The eventual polemical force should arise from that responsibility, the demonstrated deficiencies and the possibilities for construction—not from accumulating insults or extending the roster of antagonists.
