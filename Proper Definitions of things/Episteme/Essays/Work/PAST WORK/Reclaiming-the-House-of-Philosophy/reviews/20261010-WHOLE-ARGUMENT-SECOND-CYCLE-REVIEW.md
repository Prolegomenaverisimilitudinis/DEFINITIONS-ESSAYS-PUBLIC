# Whole-argument review — second cycle, 10 October 2026

Read the main working definition in full. No main or companion document edited; no web or additional agents used.

The causal sequence is now coherent as a hypothesis: authority without answerability encourages performance; performance's failed promises can become distrust of the vocation; that distrust becomes abstention; criticism can reopen the undertaking without supplying its next constructions. §§6.4–6.5 give the crucial transition an intelligible mechanism. The century-wide history remains unproved, as the document acknowledges. §11 accurately locates the immediate empirical encounter in existing knowledge, means, hardware, technologies and operative conditions. Its fabrication example explains relationships without importing a project implementation. The European/Ionian call retains force and avoids inherited authority. No universal framework prerequisite, universal Popper victory, indictment of nineteenth-century scientists, or Nietzschean rank doctrine remains.

## Priority 1 — give abandonment a bounded test, including a test of its cause

**Location:** §6.10, after the paragraph beginning “The accusation must also expose itself to contrary evidence.”

The existing paragraph rightly accepts counterexamples, but “extent, continuity and connection” and “scale proportionate” can still absorb any achievement by demanding more. More seriously, an unmet connection does not by itself establish abdication: the difficulty may require knowledge or means nobody yet possesses. Add the smallest practical test:

> For each claimed vacancy, we must identify the problem, the connection or capacity it requires, the strongest existing work addressing it, and the consequential inadequacy that remains. If existing work already supplies the alleged missing responsibility, the charge fails in that case. If the difficulty arises from unresolved knowledge or limits of means, attributing it to philosophical abdication requires further evidence.

This makes the historical accusation answerable without postponing the constructive programme. §7's “approved” example demonstrates the kind of distinction needed; it does not establish the prevalence or historical cause of such deficits. No immediate expansion into a contemporary survey is necessary for this conceptual definition.

## Priority 2 — identify the philosophical content of integration positively

**Location:** §2, after “This does not annex their entire discipline to philosophy.”

The document rejects disciplinary sovereignty but otherwise risks calling every competent engineering connection an act of reclaiming philosophy. Identify what must actually be examined:

> Connecting specialist achievements calls for philosophical work where their combination requires us to examine or revise what the subjects are, what their representations mean, what their claims establish or which purposes govern their use. Coordination under an already adequate account can exercise technical competence without undertaking that further work.

This gives “connected responsibility” substantive content. It also clarifies why specialist depth and philosophical reconstruction can cooperate without one taking ownership of the other. §§10–11 already support this distinction; the early definition should introduce it.

## Priority 3 — introduce the opening's named undertaking through its meaning

**Location:** §1, first two sentences.

The opening explains its ambition immediately, but introduces “Formal Industrial Revolution” as though the reader already knows the name. Minimal replacement:

> By a Formal Industrial Revolution, this document means the proposed transformation of our ability to understand, constitute, connect and deliberately develop the means through which civilization knows and produces. Such a transformation cannot be brought about merely by acquiring more powerful instruments.

Keep the philosophical questions that follow. A fuller account of what specifically makes this revolution formal can remain a separate development; this replacement only gives the present reader the prerequisite sense already supplied by the document.

## Lower priority — preserve local conceptual distinctions, reduce repeated source caveats

Repeated reminders that historical arguments are situated or incomplete somewhat interrupt §§4–6, but their presence is not a substantive contradiction. If tightening prose, retain the standing-of-argument paragraph, §17's historical questions and §18's source boundaries. Keep the consequential disagreements with Nietzsche at §§6.2 and 14, and the objections to philosophical sovereignty. Shorten repetitions such as §6.4's “This is a situated polemic requiring engagement with those criticized before it can become a comprehensive historical verdict” to “In this situated polemic, Popper sharpens a question for our account:” because the larger historical limit is already stated. Do not replace these repeated caveats with stronger historical assertions.

The first two corrections are the material ones. No additional metaphor, antagonist or grander historical verdict is needed for this cycle.
