# Bounded coherence and Deutsch fidelity review

Reviewed 10 October 2026. Main draft: `02-RECLAIMING-THE-HOUSE-OF-PHILOSOPHY-WORKING-DEFINITION.md`. No main edits, web research, or additional agents. References below use the main draft's current physical line numbers.

## Judgment

The conceptual spine holds: philosophical roots → creative reason answerable to reality → fracture and inhibition → available intellectual clearance → missing builders → constructive responsibility → public inheritance and summons. Section 6.7 faithfully incorporates the user's sharpening, and its distinction between intellectual clearance and universal historical disappearance prevents a false claim without canceling the analogy. Sections 9, 12, 14 and 15 carry it forward.

Deutsch fidelity is good. The draft does not annex the Diarchy to him, equate technical success with truth, treat his quantum interpretation as settled, erase Popper's constructive contribution, or present his selective history as a neutral census. The qualifications do useful conceptual work. There is no need to weaken §6.7's declaration further or insert additional general disclaimers.

The remaining consequential problem is momentum: a few later formulations return to preparing or verifying the diagnosis just after the document has said the available opening demands builders. Resolve that hierarchy while retaining the historical research obligations.

## 1. Section 7.3 unnecessarily withdraws the present charge

**Location:** line 303, following the useful distinctions at 295–301.

The line makes stagnation an eventually available term and concludes with a thesis merely to investigate. This weakens the transition: the reader has just been summoned into a cleared house, but the present constructive deficit is again presented as only a possibility. The caveat distinguishing stagnation from informal richness is already adequately established at 283 and 297–301. Keep the working charge affirmative and give later investigation a concrete task rather than suspending it.

**Suggested replacement for line 303:**

> The charge is that creative powers already available to us remain confined or insufficiently cultivated in relation to the problems we now face. This is a deficit of development, connection and exercise; it cannot be inferred merely from a practice's being informal or interpretive. The next task is to identify where those powers require builders and what those builders must make possible.

This does not claim that the selected books empirically establish the extent of the deficit. That standing is already clear at lines 11, 186–190 and 557. It restores the document's own thesis and the user's intended next question.

## 2. Section 12 should place continuing criticism inside building without reopening perpetual clearance

**Location:** line 403, particularly “complementary responsibilities rather than successive eras” and renewed weeds.

The passage is logically compatible with §6.7 but can pull the metaphor back toward permanent gardening as the central task. The latest sharpening does not require pretending errors can never recur; it requires the old occupants' disappearance to stop serving as the permanent organizing problem. Make that distinction direct.

**Suggested replacement for line 403:**

> The clearance changes the governing task: the old occupants no longer determine what happens in the house; the missing builders do. Criticism remains active within the work they undertake, testing new proposals, exposing omissions and requiring revision. It belongs to rebuilding rather than supplying a reason to remain indefinitely at the threshold.

Here the “old occupants” retain the metaphorical sense explicitly defined at 255. The passage makes no claim that all false doctrines vanished historically, and preserves Deutsch's criterion that constructive proposals remain criticizable.

## 3. Section 18 reverses the new priority

**Location:** line 559.

“Examine the proposed historical hinge and descent ... then specify the constructive responsibilities” imposes a new sequential dependency. It leaves positive work waiting upon the historical case, after §§6.7 and 12 have explained why further demolition cannot substitute for entry. Historical substantiation remains necessary, but it should support the diagnosis while the constructive question proceeds, not restore clearance as the prerequisite for everything else.

**Suggested replacement for line 559:**

> The next development is to specify the constructive responsibilities left unfulfilled: what needs to be built, which existing powers can serve it and what new means are required. Further historical work should substantiate and sharpen the genealogy while this positive account develops. Adding antagonists or repeating the case for intellectual freedom cannot perform the builders' work.

## Important unresolved support gap, without a new disclaimer

The late-eighteenth-century hinge, demographic “flight,” century of contraction and current extent of abandonment remain unsupported at the historical scale of their strongest formulations. The draft already states this clearly and puts specific research obligations in §17. No additional softening is needed. What the argument now most needs is a substantive positive case: one consequential philosophical problem, the inherited means available to address it, what those means leave missing, and the construction that would continue the work. The current “approved” distinction and production example explain possible mechanisms but do not yet establish the civilizational magnitude of the diagnosis.

The distinction is important: the sources establish ways philosophical inhibition operates and ways criticism reopens inquiry; the document proposes the wider historical diagnosis and the constructive obligation. The next source work should help make the missing builders and their tasks concrete, rather than increase the roster of former occupants.
