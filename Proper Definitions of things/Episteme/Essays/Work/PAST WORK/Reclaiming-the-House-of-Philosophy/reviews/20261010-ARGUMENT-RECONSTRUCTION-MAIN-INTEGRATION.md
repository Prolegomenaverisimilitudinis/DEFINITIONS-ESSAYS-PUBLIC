## Review findings and decisions

The whole-argument review found no consequential internal contradiction and two material explanatory defects. Both were accepted after main-agent assessment:

1. **Magnitude example:** merely saying that new means could accommodate the diagonal was insufficient. Section 7.3 now illustrates rational arithmetic enlarged to expressions of the form a + b√2, preserving old numbers and using the square-root relation in operations. It distinguishes an exact enlargement from approximation and from erasing the irrationality result. This is explicitly a modern illustration, not a claim about ancient historical sequence.
2. **Tarski/Popper attribution:** the main agent returned to Popper, chapter 10, local lines 4388–4416. Popper already accepted objective truth; his difficulty was making statement/fact correspondence clear. Section 7.4 now identifies that obstacle, explains object language and metalanguage through an illustrative truth-condition sentence, and retains truth versus acceptance as a consequence rather than misreporting it as the prior difficulty.

The bounded source reviewer initially passed the earlier Tarski formulation. The more precise finding was nevertheless confirmed directly and implemented. This is recorded because a review pass is not independent proof of the adequacy of every sentence.

Main-agent final refinements also removed a repeated determinism aside, made formal constitution versus its consequences explicit in §3.3, and developed the intellectual temptation in §6.1 without assigning universal motives.
