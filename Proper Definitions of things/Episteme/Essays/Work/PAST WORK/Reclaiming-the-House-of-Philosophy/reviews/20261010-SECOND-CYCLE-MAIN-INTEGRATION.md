## Review findings and resolutions

All material findings in the three bounded reviews were evaluated. The accepted corrections were:

1. Scope the tavern's dispersal as the decisive scene of the analogy rather than a demonstrated disappearance of every actual school.
2. Say that removing an account does not **by itself complete** an adequate successor account. Criticism can already contain constructive work.
3. Remove the stale “two philosophical books” count.
4. Make the disagreement with Nietzsche exact: his identification of philosophical creation with commanding will to power is not our constructive model.
5. Distinguish Popper's practical attitudes for discussion from compulsory shared doctrinal assumptions.
6. Correct the 1974 addendum locator to reading-copy line 1142.
7. Preserve the particular historical causes Popper attributes to the despair he discusses; do not turn the source into a biography in which obscure prose caused that despair.
8. Give the alleged abandonment a bounded test: problem, required capacity, strongest existing work, consequential gap, and evidence for abdication rather than sheer difficulty.
9. Define the philosophical content of connecting achievements, rather than annexing every successful engineering connection.
10. Introduce the proposed industrial transformation through its meaning before relying on its name.

The final pedagogical insertion was personally checked against *Conjectures and Refutations*, chapter 2, sections II–III. It preserves the useful mechanism without adopting the stronger universal thesis that every genuine philosophical question must originate outside philosophy.

## What remains
