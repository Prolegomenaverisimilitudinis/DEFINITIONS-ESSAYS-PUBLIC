# Reconstruction of the philosophical argument — main-agent working account

## Why this revision is necessary

The author's informal language indicated a direction; the previous revision treated it as selected terminology and built a dramatic sequence from it. The resulting text contained useful distinctions but let a picturesque plot substitute for the development of their relations. This revision must change the conceptual organization, not rename the scenes.

The clean MD remains the existing House of Philosophy working definition. The pre-reconstruction version is preserved in Context/06. No companion definition is editable in this task. No web research or implementation work is needed. The main agent owns the argument and personally reads, writes and verifies it; Sol 6.1 High agents investigate bounded source questions and review the candidate.

## The positive subject that must govern the diagnosis

Philosophical responsibility here concerns the examination and development of our accounts of subjects, meanings, knowledge, reasoning and purposes—including the creation of intellectual means through which previously unmanageable questions can become intelligible or answerable. This is neither a disciplinary monopoly nor ordinary coordination under already adequate premises. It includes fundamental inquiry without a known industrial use. Where operative capability is claimed, further responsibility reaches its actual realization.

The house is a restrained organizing analogy for a connected undertaking. Its alleged decline must be explained by changes in what work is considered meaningful, possible, warranted or worth attempting. Motives and professional conduct can matter, but cannot replace examining those changes.

## Required distinctions

1. Creating a conceptual or formal organization does not by itself make it an adequate account of an independent subject. Conversely, independent reality does not supply all the concepts required to understand it.
2. Examining social origins and power is not identical to reducing the validity of every claim to its social origin or efficacy.
3. Failure of certainty does not establish the impossibility of better knowledge. A system's failure does not define the limits of every successor.
4. An ill-formed expression, a false claim, an inadequate representation and an incompletely formulated genuine problem require different responses. Existing expressive means cannot automatically adjudicate all their possible successors.
5. Useful criticism, historical interpretation and scholarship become restrictive only when made the exhaustive content of the vocation or a permanent substitute for addressing its problems.
6. Immense learning requirements are a real difficulty, not evidence of pretence. Their consequences must be distinguished from fraud, relativism and withdrawal by principle.
7. These positions can conflict doctrinally while converging in effects upon certain constructive responsibilities. Such convergence does not establish a single historical succession.
8. Popper's contribution includes positive accounts of fallible knowledge, criticism and learning. The cleaner metaphor is secondary to explaining exactly which inference or prohibition his argument challenges.
9. Neither an exposed prohibition nor its institutional adoption supplies every new instrument. An unanswered problem can also reflect unresolved knowledge rather than abdication.

## Explanatory architecture

Retain a usable opening, house, Diarchy and historical setting. Replace the staged decline with differentiated arguments and their relationships. Show the constructive losses in developed cases, not only lists of concepts or small vocabulary ambiguities. Give the surviving exact/technical work a substantive role: it shows what the constructive faculty can achieve, not a token exception. Connect the present need to those losses without assuming that history's whole causal burden has been discharged. Develop the European summons from a defined responsibility rather than repetitive inheritance imagery.

The sources test and supply this account. They do not determine paragraph order by a succession of quotations. Keep attribution at the point needed to locate a contested argument; gather the larger historical limits and detailed disagreement in the source/research apparatus. Qualification must remain precise, not become the main content of every section.

## Completion tests for this revision

- Every central criticism is intelligible without the house imagery or the critic's prestige.
- The text identifies the proposition or restriction, its rationale where relevant, the disputed inference, and the constructive consequence.
- It distinguishes logical relationship, educational/institutional transmission and historical sequence.
- At least two substantial cases explain how developing an intellectual instrument changes what can be understood or done; they do not merely show coordination failure.
- The current technological encounter remains near the constructive ending, and no nineteenth-century scientist indictment is introduced.
- Europe's prospective responsibility follows from the particular capacities at stake, without a claimed monopoly of ancestry or a guaranteed historical destiny.
- The document is a coherent explanatory definition, not a theatrical essay mixed with repeated instructions to its future writer.
- Main rereading, independent review and mechanical navigation checks occur after the substantive rewrite. No completion claim substitutes for a specific resolution of discovered defects.
