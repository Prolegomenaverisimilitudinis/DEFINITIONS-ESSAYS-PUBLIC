## Source discipline and consequential repairs

- The House and father/mother Diarchy are the author's constructive interpretation. Ionia's school succession and Anaximander's reasoning are presented through Popper's explicitly conjectural reconstruction.
- Locke's fallibility/toleration role is supported in Conjectures, Introduction, pp.16–17. Rousseau's relocation of sovereignty, Fichte/Hegel and Kant's contested inheritance have distinct attributed roles, not an invented direct chain.
- Criticism of philosophical claims is not universally empirical falsification. Popper's chapter 8 explicitly permits rational assessment of nonempirical philosophical proposals by their problems and consequences.
- Source books support selected mechanisms, not every institutional prevalence claim. No invented motives, quotations or causal proof.
- Solvay retains the author's forceful comparison and expectation of increased epistemological capacity. A photograph cannot by itself establish universal participant competence or a census of living thinkers; do not invent either. Keep the comparative evidential work recoverable.
- Postmodern/post-structuralist abdication is judged by the exact exemption from truth-directed answerability charged. The supplied collection has no primary dossier establishing every named school's participation. Do not manufacture such a dossier or weaken the indictment into a generic suggestion to be careful.
- Instrumentalism uses inherited explanations even when it disavows them; predictive success is not itself abdication. The decisive charge is converting success into exemption from the remaining work.
- Computational realization is a positive refuge and a productive means for further means. Its section must reach its own affirmative conclusion before the next problem is developed.
- The probabilistic-engine example receives only the space needed to expose a contemporary symptom: delegated performance mistaken for release from creative responsibility. Its usefulness is not on trial, and the account does not turn into a technical argument about systems design or model control.
