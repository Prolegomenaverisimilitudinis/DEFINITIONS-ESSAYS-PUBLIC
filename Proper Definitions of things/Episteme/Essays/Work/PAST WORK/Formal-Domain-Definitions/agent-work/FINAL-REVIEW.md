# Bounded final review of the 9 October candidate

Reviewed the complete goal, the candidate introduction and §§1–5 for context, and §§6–19 in full. This review owns the latter sections. It does not assess the opening source interpretations assigned to the other reviewer and does not constitute final acceptance.

## Judgment

The reviewed account is substantively adequate to the assigned conceptual obligations. I found no consequential contradiction or must-fix mathematical error in §§6–19. The direct definition-to-operation claim is positive and appropriately conditional: §15 distinguishes reusable realizing powers from the particular domain commitments, gives preservation and realization obligations, separates missing semantics from missing operative capacity, and does not claim that a universal environment currently exists. §§16–17 preserve the difference between adequate definition, supported realization, execution, external grounds and purpose achievement. Essential semantics are not delegated to unspecified future mathematics.

The boundary account in §6.4, composite laws in §7, context and joint-relation account in §8, transformation/result distinctions in §§9–10, and change distinctions in §11 form a connected explanation. They do not assume that a combination of locally adequate pieces is automatically adequate. §§12–13 correctly distinguish finite specification from finite possibilities and necessary recursive discovery from automatic termination. Mathematics is introduced through concrete responsibilities rather than being made the agenda.

## Optional improvement 1 — §15.1: show one actual instance of the positive derivation relationship

**Status:** Optional; strengthens independent intelligibility, not a missing central premise.

**Ground:** §15.1 explains the relationship clearly, but its positive claim remains abstract. The exact composite already defined in §7 supplies a compact demonstration without requiring an implementation specification. A reader can then see what is supplied by a particular definition and what is already supplied by the reusable environment.

**Smallest useful correction:** Add a short paragraph such as:

> For the finite construction in §7, an environment that faithfully realizes finite families, maps, image formation and fiber access can derive the selected units \(U\), their support families \(S_u\), and the declared accessors from the definition and its represented population. The domain definition supplies why an assembly–component pair is the selected unit and which access each responsibility requires; the environment supplies reusable means for exercising those specified constructions. A new population changes the resulting units and supports without requiring their domain meanings to be invented again.

This describes a conditional example, not a claim about today's machinery. It should not be expanded into a general runtime design.

## Optional improvement 2 — §15.3: state the initial correspondence premise explicitly

**Status:** Optional mathematical precision; the intended premise is readily inferable from the surrounding text.

**Ground:** The paragraph introduces \(\alpha(s,r)\) and then describes a transition from \(r\), but does not explicitly say that the chosen starting states satisfy that relation. A correspondence obligation must start from related states, rather than an arbitrary represented state \(s\).

**Smallest useful correction:** Change the sentence opening to:

> Whenever \(\alpha(s,r)\) holds, if the operative arrangement performs a meaningful transition \(r\leadsto r'\), faithful behavior requires …

The existing explanation of internal steps and the additional direction required for a capability claim can remain.
