# Generative constitution through typed operations, equations and interpretation

Investigation date: 9 October 2026. Bounded mathematical investigation; no change to the clean definition. The construction below is an illustrative proposal, not a reconstruction of an established domain's entire practice.

## Question and finding

The present definition's §§7–16 explain that objects need connecting laws and that activities need meaningful interfaces, but their developed mathematics stops primarily at finite families, projections and interval checks. Can a finite account constitute an indefinitely extensible family of constructions, with a proved relationship between each composite construction and its operative interpretation?

Yes, for a bounded class of typed, total, immutable constructions: a sorted signature supplies primitive formation and access; equations determine which composites agree; a term algebra generates all finite constructions; interpretations assign carriers and primitive operations; structural induction extends that assignment to every generated term. The same induction establishes preservation through composition. The missing bridge is therefore not a separate implementation invented for every composite. It is the prior constitution and faithful realization of the primitive meanings, together with the admitted rules of composition.

This does not establish that all domain work has this character. It also does not make structural equations sufficient for judging empirical or interpretive adequacy. A deliberately defective interpretation below satisfies every interface equation while misjudging the candidate. That countermodel locates the insufficiency exactly.

## A concrete responsibility with a consequential internal interface

Consider evaluating a candidate revision under two declared requirements, retaining its assessments and grounds, and repeating after revisions. The candidate could be an engineering proposal. The modest responsibility here is to generate and evaluate finite revision–assessment constructions over an already constituted, frozen input organization.

An ordinary account might say: “revise the proposal, assess both requirements, combine the results.” A competent practitioner ensures that both assessments concern the same candidate revision, the same adopted requirements, and compatible assumptions. They also recognize that assessment of the previous revision cannot automatically be reused. The activity names do not themselves make that recognition a property of every generated composite.

We put the shared subject into a request object. A request includes a candidate revision, both versioned criteria, the evidence snapshot or formal input state, and the adopted assessment conventions. Request equality means equality of this complete represented subject for the present responsibility. Equality of candidate names is weaker. This is a modelling decision justified by the use of results together; it is not an assertion that all of those fields must always be physically duplicated in each record.

For the mathematical example, requests and results are immutable, and each chosen primitive denotes a total function over the admitted carriers. A revision produces a candidate description, which can fail its requirements. It does not promise to produce a physically constructible or satisfactory candidate. If a selected edit cannot be represented for every request, or an assessment can remain unavailable, this signature needs a typed refusal/availability result or a restricted formation account. Neither difficulty is solved by calling the operation total.

The generic construction does not yet establish what the criteria mean or why the assessment procedures are appropriate. The concrete numerical interpretation below supplies one restricted answer, and exposes what remains outside it.

## Objects, operations and laws

Use the seven sorts

\[
Q,\ E,\ A_1,\ A_2,\ P,\ D,\ V.
\]

They denote request, edit, assessment for requirement 1, assessment for requirement 2, coherent assessment package, supported decision, and verdict. In this example a verdict can include favorable, adverse, or unresolved; a runtime failure is not silently one of these judgments. The particular outcome vocabulary must be defined for the adopted responsibility.

Primitive operations are:

| Operation | Type | Meaning in this illustration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `revise` | \(Q\times E\to Q\) | Form a revised candidate request under the adopted conventions |
| `assess_i` | \(Q\to A_i\) | Form the declared requirement assessment for that request |
| `subject_i` | \(A_i\to Q\) | Recover the complete subject of that assessment |
| `package` | \(Q\to P\) | Form the two assessments as a coherent composite |
| `request` | \(P\to Q\) | Recover the package's subject |
| `part_i` | \(P\to A_i\) | Access each internal assessment |
| `decide` | \(P\to D\) | Form a decision under the declared reconciliation procedure |
| `grounds` | \(D\to P\) | Retain the package supporting the decision |
| `verdict` | \(D\to V\) | Access the resulting judgment |

For \(i=1,2\), impose the following equations, with variables explicitly quantified at the indicated sorts:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\forall q:Q,\quad
 &\operatorname{subject}_i(\operatorname{assess}_i(q))=q,\\
 &\operatorname{request}(\operatorname{package}(q))=q,\\
 &\operatorname{part}_i(\operatorname{package}(q))=\operatorname{assess}_i(q);\\
\forall p:P,\quad
 &\operatorname{subject}_i(\operatorname{part}_i(p))=\operatorname{request}(p),\\
 &\operatorname{grounds}(\operatorname{decide}(p))=p.
\end{aligned}
\]

These are imposed interface commitments. They say that the two parts of every admitted package have its subject, and that generated decisions retain their package. They are not deductions from the words “assessment” and “decision.” Nor are they statements that every assessment is accurate, or that a favorable verdict follows.

There is intentionally no unconstrained constructor \(Q\times A_1\times A_2\to P\). Such a constructor could package mismatched revisions unless the signature also specified how it checks correspondence and returns failure. Existing independent assessments can still be represented in their sorts. Importing them into a coherent package is a separate responsibility: either provide the equalities of complete subject, use a context-indexed formation rule, or define an explicit validation operation. Ordinary type agreement does not discharge it.

This example makes a composite object more than an unrelated tuple: its constituents have projections and common-subject laws which every admitted inhabitant must respect. It also preserves grounds as a reusable internal interface, rather than flattening the decision to a Boolean.

## What is generated, rather than enumerated

A finite input context assigns sorts to variables, for example

\[
\Gamma=(q:Q,e_1:E,e_2:E).
\]

A variable is a term of its assigned sort. Applying a primitive to terms of its input sorts forms a term of its output sort. Thus

\[
d=\operatorname{decide}(\operatorname{package}(
\operatorname{revise}(\operatorname{revise}(q,e_1),e_2)))
\]

is a generated term of sort \(D\), rather than an item that had to occur in an inventory. Its subject, its two assessment constituents, its grounds, and its verdict can be accessed through further terms.

For any finite number \(n\) of supplied edits, recursively define

\[
q_0=q,\qquad q_{j+1}=\operatorname{revise}(q_j,e_{j+1}),
\qquad d_j=\operatorname{decide}(\operatorname{package}(q_j)).
\]

This is a family of arbitrarily deep finite constructions generated by a finite signature. It is not a promise to finish an infinite execution. Even one repeated edit variable gives terms of unbounded depth.

The equations prove, for each generated \(d_j\),

\[
\operatorname{request}(\operatorname{grounds}(d_j))=q_j,
\qquad
\operatorname{subject}_i(\operatorname{part}_i(\operatorname{grounds}(d_j)))=q_j.
\]

The first follows by the grounds and request laws. The second follows either through the package law and assessment law or directly through the package's common-subject law. The result is a reusable guarantee over the whole generated family: the assessments supporting a decision concern that decision's generated request. No separate proof or hand-written bridge is required for every length of revision history.

Different syntax need not mean different constructions. For example

\[
\operatorname{revise}(\operatorname{request}(\operatorname{package}(q)),e)
=\operatorname{revise}(q,e)
\]

is derived by substituting the request equation into an enclosing operation. Equating such terms uses the smallest congruence generated by the imposed equations: it respects input substitution and replacement within larger constructions. An additional equation cannot be added merely because it makes normalization convenient. In particular, edit order is not assumed commutative.

## Why individual validity does not settle the whole construction

Take two individually correct assessments

\[
a_1=\operatorname{assess}_1(q),\qquad
a_2=\operatorname{assess}_2(\operatorname{revise}(q,e)).
\]

They have the required ordinary sorts \(A_1,A_2\). They can nevertheless be the wrong joint basis for assessing one revision. The first subject is \(q\); the second is \(\operatorname{revise}(q,e)\). Nothing in the declared theory proves them equal.

A concrete witness makes the failure visible. Let the original represented proposal have mass 8 and clearance demand 12, with limits 10 and 10. It passes the mass requirement and fails clearance. Let an edit produce mass 12 and clearance demand 8. That revision fails mass and passes clearance. Combining the original mass pass with the revised clearance pass makes an apparent double pass that neither revision satisfies.

This is a structural composition error even when both individual numerical checks are correct. The missing constituent is the jointly bound revision and assessment package. Adding an arrow labelled “combine” between outputs does not supply it.

The imposed package laws exclude that pair when the two requests are distinct: an alleged package with these parts would force both requests to equal its single request. The distinction of revisions must itself survive interpretation. A model that identifies all requests can satisfy the equations while destroying their intended use; the laws do not prove the required identity discrimination on their own.

## Actual category and its useful work

The term account has a precise category \(T\):

- Objects are finite lists of sorts, interpreted as input/output contexts.
- A morphism \((s_1,\ldots,s_m)\to(t_1,\ldots,t_n)\) is an \(n\)-tuple of terms of the respective output sorts, using variables of the input sorts, modulo the generated equations.
- Identity morphisms are the input variables in order.
- Composition substitutes an upstream tuple into a downstream tuple.
- Products concatenate contexts; projections select variables; the empty context is terminal.

Associativity and identity of composition follow from substitution. The congruence guarantees that replacing representatives by equal terms does not change a composite. This is the relevant sorted algebraic theory, not a category inferred from a picture of activities.

For example \(F:QE\to Q\) is the term `revise(q,e)`, and \(G:Q\to D\) is `decide(package(q))`. Their composite is precisely `decide(package(revise(q,e)))`. A product-preserving interpretation \(M:T\to\mathbf{Set}\) assigns sets to the sorts and functions to the primitives, satisfies the equations, and therefore evaluates every generated construction. Preservation of composition means

\[
M(G\circ F)=M(G)\circ M(F).
\]

The benefit is exact: separate realization of lawful primitives determines realization of their generated composites. This is one mathematical basis for direct derivation within an admitted language of definitions.

Products here reuse immutable information. They permit duplicating a request variable and discarding an unused assessment reference. They are not permission to copy a scarce resource, erase a required observation event, or duplicate a human's participation. If such effects are consequential, a Cartesian term theory is insufficient for those actions. One must constitute the relevant behavioral/resource account instead of silently treating it as pure information.

## Interpretation supplies substantive commitments which syntax does not

Here is an explicit numerical interpretation demonstrating consistency. Let a request be

\[
q=(\rho,m,w,L_m,L_w),
\]

where \(\rho\) is an immutable revision description, \(m,w\) are represented mass and clearance demand, and \(L_m,L_w\) are the two limits under fixed declared units and conventions. The carriers use rational nonnegative quantities. For this tiny interpretation, an edit is a pair of new rational nonnegative mass and demand values; `revise` appends that edit to \(\rho\), assigns those values to \(m,w\), and retains the limits. This particular total edit operation is an illustrative choice, not an account of every engineering change. It represents a proposed numerical revision; it does not assert that a physical design producing those values exists.

Set

\[
A_i=Q\times\{\mathrm{pass},\mathrm{fail}\},
\]

with `subject_i` the first projection. Define

\[
\operatorname{assess}_1(q)=(q,[m\le L_m]),\quad
\operatorname{assess}_2(q)=(q,[w\le L_w]),
\]

where the bracket returns pass or fail. Let

\[
P=\{(q,a_1,a_2):\operatorname{subject}_1(a_1)
=q=\operatorname{subject}_2(a_2)\}.
\]

`package` constructs the triple containing both declared assessments; `request` and `part_i` project it. Set \(D=P\times V\), make `grounds` the first projection, and define `decide(p)` to return \((p,\mathrm{favorable})\) exactly when both constituent judgments are pass, otherwise \((p,\mathrm{adverse})\). The unused unresolved verdict can remain available in \(V\); totality over this specific numerical input organization does not require all verdicts to occur.

All eight interface equations now hold by calculation. The numerical inequalities and the conjunction rule were supplied in the interpretation. They are not consequences of the eight interface equations. Given this interpreted numerical model, generated decisions evaluate those comparisons, retain the joint subject, and retain their grounds.

A second interpretation keeps the same carriers, package projections and grounds, but lets `assess_1` compare clearance demand against its clearance limit, exactly as `assess_2` does. Both assessments still refer to the complete, correct request. All eight interface equations still hold. For mass 12, clearance demand 8, and both limits 10, it returns two passes and a favorable decision. The intended interpretation returns a mass failure and an adverse decision. The defect is not stale identity or broken wiring: the primitive assigned to the mass-assessment role performs the wrong substantive comparison. Merely retaining the criterion's name in the request does not repair this.

To exclude that interpretation, the domain constitution must supply which represented quantity the first role concerns, how it is obtained, which represented criterion constrains it, which unit/convention and qualifiers govern comparison, what counts as meeting that criterion, and what the assessment result asserts. The concrete numerical interpretation supplied that content through \([m\le L_m]\) under fixed units and limits. A richer definition might constitute the quantities, dimensions, comparisons and justification themselves as further operations and laws. That is a recursive development of the primitive meanings, not a proof that these eight structural equations were already sufficient.

There is another admission distinction: the chosen carriers \(A_i=Q\times\{\mathrm{pass},\mathrm{fail}\}\) also contain pairs which were not produced by the declared assessor. The set \(P\) enforces common subject, not truth of an arbitrary imported judgment. The generated packages `package(q)` in the good interpretation use the declared procedures. Claiming the same adequacy for an arbitrary supplied variable \(p:P\) additionally requires an admission or authenticity account, restricting allowed values, preserving a derivation, or validating the imported assessments. A sort named “assessment” cannot by itself make every inhabitant a justified assessment. This separates a generated construction's guarantees from the trust owed to externally supplied operands.

A third, faithful realization could encode requests and packages as immutable stored objects with exact lookup/access functions rather than tuples. A sortwise bijection transporting all operations produces an isomorphic algebra with the same observable judgments. Thus even once primitive meaning is settled, there need not be one physical representation. Lawful realizations can differ in encoding; structurally lawful interpretations can also differ in substantive meaning. Those are different forms of plurality.

Represented mass is not automatically actual mass. Relating it to a physical proposal requires an agreed measurement or modelling procedure, revision correspondence, units, tolerances, evidence, and applicability of the adopted comparison. Those obligations cannot be inferred from the term `assess_1(q)` or from the interpretation functor. In a psychological or interpretive domain the corresponding grounding can include a construct, an operationalization or an adopted analytical account, with different obligations. This example does not settle them by analogy.

## What a map between interpretations preserves

For two algebras \(M,N\), a homomorphism is a family \(h_s:M_s\to N_s\) satisfying, for every primitive \(\sigma:s_1\cdots s_k\to s\),

\[
h_s(\sigma^M(x_1,\ldots,x_k))
=\sigma^N(h_{s_1}(x_1),\ldots,h_{s_k}(x_k)).
\]

This condition preserves all generated terms, not merely the named primitives. Proof by induction: it holds for assigned variables; assuming it holds for each operand, the displayed preservation equation establishes it for applying a primitive. Consequently

\[
h_s(\llbracket t\rrbracket_M(\gamma))
=\llbracket t\rrbracket_N(h\gamma)
\]

for every generated term \(t\) of sort \(s\) and input assignment \(\gamma\). This is the substantive preservation theorem needed for reuse and translation.

But a homomorphism need not be injective: it may merge different requests. Equations alone even admit the singleton algebra of every sort. If a receiving use must distinguish revisions, correspondence needs injectivity on the relevant subjects or a stronger declared adequacy condition. If it must distinguish favorable from adverse, the verdict correspondence must preserve that distinction as well. A useful lossy map is not automatically an adequate substitute for the richer realization.

## Equations can support computation, with a bounded proof

Orient the eight displayed equations left to right. Every rewrite decreases the total number of operation-symbol occurrences. None duplicates a variable. Hence no infinite rewrite sequence exists.

The only overlap between left sides at a nonvariable proper subterm is

\[
\operatorname{subject}_i(\operatorname{part}_i(\operatorname{package}(q))).
\]

Reducing the outer common-subject rule gives `request(package(q))`, then \(q\). Reducing `part_i(package(q))` first gives `subject_i(assess_i(q))`, then \(q\). The two reductions join. Distinct root patterns do not overlap; reductions in disjoint subterms commute; reductions inside a substituted variable commute with the enclosing rule because that variable is retained exactly once. Therefore all one-step divergences join. Induction on the maximum remaining reduction length now gives confluence: for two reduction paths, join their initial steps and apply the inductive hypothesis to the smaller descendants. Each term consequently has one normal form, and equation equality for this restricted presentation can be checked by comparing normal forms.

This is an independently established property of this exact eight-rule presentation. It is not a general assertion that domain equations can always be made terminating or confluent. Additional domain equations require a new argument. Also normalization of an accessor expression proves interface identities; it does not perform the actual assessment or establish physical adequacy. Erasing an assessment subterm under an accessor is justified here by the declared pure, total interpretation, not by an assumption that an effectful assessment event may be skipped in ordinary practice.

## Concrete passage to operative realization

For this bounded class, a reusable environment must supply typed term formation, variable binding and substitution, composite access, and faithful evaluation of the admitted primitive functions. A domain definition supplies the carriers' admitted meanings, identity conditions, primitive meanings, and equations. Inputs supply actual represented requests and edits. Evaluation of the generated two-revision term then derives the requested arrangement of revision, assessment, package formation and decision from the definition; no new semantic invention is needed for that composite.

To claim the intended capability, the following must actually be established:

1. The request organization distinguishes each context and revision on which the assessment depends, with evidence about its relationship to the external subject where relevant.
2. `revise` has the promised admitted inputs and effects. In the numerical illustration it constructs a revised description, not a built object.
3. Each realized assessment performs its declared substantive procedure and preserves its complete subject. The equations establish the second obligation; they do not establish the first.
4. The package and decision realizations satisfy the interface and grounding laws, and the verdict procedure has its declared interpretation.
5. The evaluator preserves operation application and equations throughout generated composites, retains required identities, and has the promised behavior for the admitted finite terms.

If an assessment requires a human or learned contribution, that contribution's subject, requested judgment, accepted grounds, returned representation and unresolved outcomes must enter the constitution. Treating an unpredictable contribution as a pure total function without an account of its response and scope would change the present example's premises. This investigation does not license that move.

## Established, proposed and unresolved

**Established within the stated construction:** a finite sorted signature generates terms of unbounded finite depth; the imposed equations prove the common-subject and retained-grounds properties for all generated decision terms; the category of contexts and terms composes by substitution; primitive-preserving maps preserve every generated term; the exact oriented eight-rule system terminates and is confluent; explicit good and defective models demonstrate that structural lawfulness does not determine substantive assessment adequacy.

**Imposed by the example:** immutable total operations, the chosen request identity, the seven sorts, two assessment roles, shared-subject package laws, retained grounds, and the numerical model's procedures. None is a universal ontology or a consequence of equational algebra alone.

**Unresolved for actual domains:** which request distinctions and primitive procedures are adequate; their empirical or interpretive grounds; partiality, interaction and live change where relevant; independent-assessment import; physical/resource effects; and the actual reusable environment's implementation and verification. These are concrete obligations, not a general disclaimer replacing the positive bridge.

The main corrective for the clean definition is that generative constitution specifies both a language of admissible constructions and an interpretation which respects its formation and laws. The mathematical induction explains why definition can determine arbitrarily many composites from a finite primitive account. The countermodel explains why that achievement cannot manufacture the primitive meanings, their external grounds, or the identity distinctions required for the claimed domain work.
