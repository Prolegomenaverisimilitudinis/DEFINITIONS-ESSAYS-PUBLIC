## Discovery 1: existing semantics is not merely implicit software

The old account began too readily from objects already recognized. Actual sources use conceptual relations to constitute what a reader recognizes: enjoyment has an object; explanation has a target question; an event has an interpreted role; fit concerns an ensemble. These are not optional metadata attached to a self-explanatory record.

Examples teach distinctions by relying on prior language, causal knowledge, intentions and context. A presentation can be meaningful and stable to its audience without fully constituting the responsibilities a different realization would need. This is deeper than ambiguity and more specific than saying cognition is tacit.

Draft consequences: rewritten §§2–3 and actual comparative investigations §4; definition/ontology §5 revised accordingly.

## Discovery 2: one meaning can join another and create a new constructive subject

Keller's relevance discussion requires distinguishing personal value, topic association and contribution to an instructional objective. Connecting them creates the subject of a reasoned instructional plan. Evaluating the result adds another relationship: what later enjoyment or performance evidence actually concerns.

A reused topic label cannot discharge these obligations. A proposed plan can specify meaningful component roles and their joining grounds without guaranteeing learning. This separates constitutive design from empirical achievement without denying constructive capability.

Draft consequences: §4.3 follows meaning to arrangement and evaluation; §15 explains the actual realization boundary.

## Discovery 3: interpretation affects ontology, not merely final labels

Altman's examples show why action types without bearers can lose the narrative contrast an interpretation needs. Changing the analytical question changes which represented subjects and relations are consequential. The alternative reading is not adopted as unquestionable truth.

Deci/Ryan similarly distinguish a report occurrence, its interpreted referent, evidence use and construct attribution. Changing interpretation can alter downstream conclusions while preserving the raw report.

## Primary mathematical construction: question-sensitive sufficiency

For cases W, representation p:W→X and determinate question q:W→Y, an answer function on p(W) exists exactly when equal p-images imply equal q-answers. Necessity is functional equality; sufficiency defines qbar(p(w))=q(w), well-defined by the premise.

For question family Q, w~Qv iff all questions agree. Representation adequacy requires ker(p)⊆~Q. Scope expansion can refine the needed discrimination. This is a direct elementary argument, not a literature claim of a newly discovered theorem.

Meaning: if a score record discards the report's referent, no process on that record alone can always recover the activity-versus-reward distinction. External/contextual/learned contributions are genuinely additional. This does not make a person's motivation determinate from one referent.

A complementary pullback A×K B represents same-context assessment pairs with projections and universal factorization. Exact common context cannot rescue an inadequate K or establish truth of judgments. This differentiates algebraic structure from domain adequacy.
