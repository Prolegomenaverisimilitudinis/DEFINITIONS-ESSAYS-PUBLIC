## 3. What changed intellectually

The earlier account requested several responsibilities without sufficiently constituting them. The revision starts from the meaning already present in domain practice.

A domain's communicated account can be meaningful and reliable for competent participants while depending on subjects, distinctions, contextual bindings and inferential habits not supplied in the account itself. That is more consequential than natural-language ambiguity. The new account reconstructs such dependencies before proposing formal replacements or representations.

A second discovery is that interpretation can change ontology: determining the referent of a report or the bearer of a narrative action changes what the domain must retain as a subject. Assigning existing labels to nodes would not capture this.

A third is that representation adequacy depends on intended questions. The exact factorization argument identifies when omitted distinctions make a question impossible to answer from the representation alone. It does not discover the question or turn its answer into an algorithm.

A fourth is that interface lawfulness and semantic adequacy are distinct. The assessment algebra has both intended and defective interpretations satisfying the same binding/interface equations. The defective assessor examines the wrong quantity. Thus correct composition can preserve a wrong primitive meaning; the primitive interpretation itself needs grounds.

The positive consequence is also developed: once the relevant meanings and primitive realizations are warranted, finite defining forms can determine arbitrarily many finite composites, with preservation justified by structural induction. This is a genuine productive possibility, not a claim that any declaration creates whatever capability it names.

## 5. Mathematical achievements and precise limits

- Representation factorization: an answer function on retained representations exists exactly when cases with equal retained representations have equal answers, under the stated function premises. Existence is not computability.
- Pullback: the same-context pair has the stated projections and universal property. It enforces equality in the chosen context representation, not adequacy of that representation or truth of assessments.
- Sorted algebra: eight distinct interface equations are well-typed; generated request/constituent invariants follow at every finite revision depth.
- Context category: finite sort contexts, term tuples modulo congruence, identities and substitution define the categorical composition used in the explanation.
- Countermodels: retaining common-subject structure does not certify the assessor's intended criterion; generated packages and arbitrary imported packages have different assurances.
- Preservation: a sorted homomorphism preserves all generated terms by structural induction. Noninjective preservation can lose distinctions needed by a later question.
- Gluing: compatible feature assignments glue uniquely by coordinate. Additional global admissibility constraints do not follow from raw gluing.

The algebra's pure, total, immutable-information premises are explicit. It is not generalized without argument to physical resources, partial observations or stateful transformations. Sheaf/topos references do not establish an automatic bridge for arbitrary domains.
