## Judgment

This is a substantive change in explanatory achievement, not an expansion of topic coverage alone. The enjoyment investigation actually reconstructs an interpretive contribution; the narrative case demonstrates a consequential choice of analytical subject; the mathematical constructions distinguish retained bindings from correct primitive interpretation and establish reusable compositional results. The positive possibility of direct operability survives the external-validity qualifications.

I would not yet call the governing goal completed. One consequential repair remains in the richest constructive investigation: the instructional case diagnoses meaningful relevance and gives necessary plan constituents, but still does not develop an actual connecting construction sufficiently to demonstrate how an interpreted meaning becomes a productive operand. The required repair is bounded. It does not require a complete theory of teaching, content comprehension, motivation or narrative quality, nor a universal realization environment.

## Findings

### P1 — The constructive instructional bridge still delegates its decisive content to “a justified connection”

**Exact location:** §4.3, especially candidate lines 284–300. Decisive sentence at line 294:

> “A bridge from a learner-valued purpose to the objective needs an intelligible intermediate achievement and a justified connection.”

Its downstream counterpart is §15.1, line 1113:

> “The latter contribution must be supplied, with an interpreted subject, grounds and limits that the other constructions can use.”

**Why consequential:** The investigation now establishes an important negative distinction: sharing a hurricane topic cannot discharge an explanatory obligation. It also identifies whose purposes matter and separates introductory, explanatory and assessment roles. But the positive bridge still consists of named responsibilities. Nothing yet shows what the connecting argument contains, how its components are distinguished, what makes one component usable by another, or which operative step constructs a new permitted plan from those meanings.

This matters especially because the governing goal explicitly rejects defining meaning by saying that interpretation or an intermediate must be supplied. Sections 3.2 and 8.5 overcome that problem for an enjoyment report's referent. Section 14 overcomes it for an immutable numerical request. The richer purpose–content–activity construction remains short of the same explanatory standard.

**Concrete challenge:** A plan can record a learner's concern about safety, attach a damage photograph, name a formation objective, label a later diagram “explanation,” and attach an assessment to that diagram. It can satisfy every listed role and retain every binding while failing to specify any formation relationship actually communicated by the diagram. It can even include a written bridge saying that formation knowledge is relevant to safety. That is a proposed relation, not yet the connection's constitution. Alternatively, it can use formation knowledge as if it already licensed a practical safety or forecasting conclusion. Correct objective–component bindings do not exclude this scope substitution. The candidate itself teaches exactly this difference between correct connections and correct primitive meaning in §14.5; the instructional case needs its corresponding positive construction.

**Smallest substantive repair:** Extend §4.3 by one bounded, worked lesson fragment, and let §15.1 refer to its resulting responsibility. This can remain a conceptual construction rather than an implementation specification:

1. Select one explicitly adopted explanatory achievement within the hurricane-formation objective. State the relationships it requires; do not let “explain formation” serve as the entire content.
2. Show an actual content interpretation: which depicted or stated elements stand for which subjects, which relationship a learner is asked to recognize, and which surrounding explanation is necessary. Distinguish a represented precursor, a temporal ordering, a causal claim, and a destructive consequence wherever the particular fragment uses them. A temporal picture does not establish causation merely through an arrow.
3. Show the bridge's intermediate result in words: the learner-valued concern motivates a particular question, that question calls for the selected explanatory relationship, and a specified activity elicits or exercises that relationship. State why the activity is relevant to the adopted objective without claiming it guarantees the learner's interest or practical safety.
4. Specify one permitted composition and one rejected or unresolved composition. For example, a response identifying only damage can be adequate for an adopted damage-identification task but inadequate evidence for the formation relationship; a changed objective can make the same photograph suitable in a different role.
5. Give the human content interpreter a bounded return: interpreted claims and bindings, supporting passages or diagram conventions, unresolved readings, and a scope of use. Show how those results enter plan formation or checking. The interpreter need not be replaced by an algorithm, but the contribution should be concretely inspectable.

These five items can fit in several developed paragraphs or one compact example. They would demonstrate a positive meaning-to-construction chain without asserting a validated pedagogical model. Do not add an unsupported hurricane mechanism merely to fill the gap; distinguish an adopted illustrative account from sourced empirical claims.

**Source check:** I inspected Keller's actual chapter-5 passages at local source lines 2149–2171 and 2393–2440, corresponding to the stated pp. 98–100 and 115–116. They support the candidate's distinction between learner-perceived value and instructional contribution. They also distinguish a destruction image from an image of a tropical-storm precursor, and discuss explanatory drawings. They do not supply a general safety bridge or establish that a coherent bridge guarantees learning. The repair should preserve the candidate's current restraint on those claims.

**Completion consequence:** Until repaired, the candidate shows necessary ontology and excludes a substitution, but its principal semantically rich constructive case still partly promises the intellectual work rather than performing it. Repair this before using the candidate to demonstrate the goal's connected-investigation and deeper-constitution requirements.

### P2 — Analogical transfer and stabilization through practice are only lightly explained

**Exact location:** §§2.3–2.4 explain examples, diagram conventions and distributed contributions; §3.4, line 187, characterizes the “human compiler” analogy. Section 19's existing-semantic-organization row states that examples and practices make concepts usable. No passage examines how an analogy transfers some relationships while excluding others, or how a community's practice stabilizes an application without making it a fully explicit formal rule.

**Why consequential:** The central account of current semantic organization is stronger than a natural-language-ambiguity diagnosis. However, its most developed case is referential interpretation. The governing goal asks for concepts becoming meaningful through examples, analogies, conventions, and practices of learning, dispute and revision. These mechanisms should not collapse into the single fact that a reader supplies context. Analogical transfer can generate a new understanding; correction in practice can change what counts as an admissible application. They are different contributions.

**Smallest substantive repair:** Add one short worked explanation to §§2.3–3.4. The existing human-compiler analogy can serve without another source programme: identify the relationship transferred from compilation (an expressed organization becomes an exercised arrangement), identify what cannot be transferred (a fully fixed source semantics and deterministic translation), and explain why competent domain interpretation can instead supply, challenge or create commitments absent from its presentation. Then add one concrete correction cycle drawn from an existing case: a practitioner identifies a topic match, a contrastive example reveals the missing explanatory role, and subsequent use stabilizes the distinction between introduction and explanation. This supplies an actual learning/revision relation rather than another list of capacities.

**Completion consequence:** This is a narrower coverage weakness than P1. It should not reopen a project to explain all cognition. A bounded explanation would make the opening's general semantic account better proportioned to the concrete investigations.

## What succeeds, with concrete grounds

### Existing meaning and trained interpretation

Sections 1–2 correctly preserve fields and domains before capability design. They do not equate a domain with a database, implementation or purpose package. Section 2.2 distinguishes referential, contrastive, inferential, explanatory, functional, normative and constructive contributions without claiming a universal semantic taxonomy.

Section 3.2 is the strongest semantic trace. An enjoyment answer acquires an object, is related to an episode and inquiry, and enters a theoretical interpretation only after the activity/reward distinction has been retained. The conclusion changes under a plausible interpretation of the same recorded answer. Clarification creates further work and preserves its relationship to the original evidence. These are actual dependencies, not names for mental faculties. The functional reconstruction is expressly distinguished from a claim about a person's conscious sequence or exact psychological mechanism.

The narrative case also makes a substantive ontological point: action types, action occurrences and participants cannot be collapsed without losing the reversal an interpretation concerns. The atmosphere/action contrast supplies an internal variation within interpretive work. The account does not declare one analysis the universal ontology of narrative.

### Comparison and empirical discipline

Sections 2.5 and 4 show differing combinations of exact procedures and interpretive work rather than ranking whole disciplines. Sorted algebra has explicit formation and equations; its application owes a domain interpretation. Psychology distinguishes a construct, operational indicators and qualified evidence. Engineering preserves local exactness while diagnosing assumptions and downstream use. Narrative permits explicit constraints and competing interpretations.

The document consistently distinguishes reconstruction, empirical inquiry, adopted requirement and constitutive decision. The enjoyment case does not turn a clarified report into proof of intrinsic motivation. The lesson case does not turn a semantic criterion into a universal empirical rule about dramatic illustrations. Section 5.5 allows representations of failure instead of defining adverse cases out of existence. These are significant strengths to preserve during repair.

### Mathematics doing intellectual work

Sections 8.5 and 8.6 have definite mathematical jobs: the factorization condition explains which lost distinctions cannot be recovered from a representation alone; the pullback distinguishes a common-subject pair from two unbound assessments. These support the domain account rather than decorate it.

Section 14 does more than name an algebra or category. It defines sorts, operations, equations, generated terms, substitution, interpretation and a structural preservation argument. Its wrong-assessor model is particularly useful: all interface laws can hold while the domain's primitive judgment is wrong. The text consequently does not equate formal well-formedness with substantive adequacy.

The construction exceeds the previous counting/interval illustrations in its definition of an unbounded term family and preservation across composition. Its claim is modest but positive: meaningful primitive interpretations and a faithful evaluator can supply new finite composites without fresh downstream semantic invention. This review does not independently certify every algebraic-theory convention; the principal agent's mathematical audit remains relevant.

The feature-assignment gluing example has actual restrictions and a uniqueness argument. The parity warning identifies an additional global constraint that local agreement does not discharge. Dependent types, graph transformations and Caramello bridges are less developed directions, but the substantial algebraic investigation means they need not each become a tutorial to meet the finite document's purpose.

### Human contribution and direct operability

Sections 3.5–3.6, 6.4 and 15.1 explicitly preserve human participation and distributed means. They do not magically replace observation, content interpretation, causal inquiry or discretionary judgment with an “intelligent interpreter.” The remaining issue is to demonstrate one of those bounded contributions concretely in the constructive case, as P1 requests.

Sections 14.3–14.6 and 15 make a positive productive claim despite the caveats. Finite defining forms generate indefinitely many finite constructions; justified primitive preservation propagates to their composites. The distinction between deriving an apparatus, performing work and achieving an external purpose is maintained. The fixed immutable scope is a premise of one demonstration, not presented as the boundary of future domain definition.

## Whole-document sequence and remaining scope

The sequence is coherent: existing domains and their semantics precede intended constitution; contrasting cases precede the general ontology; context and interfaces precede the mathematical construction; mathematical interpretation precedes derived operability. I found no decisive stale section crossreference or contradiction in the complete candidate. Sections 14–15 accurately refer back to the earlier cases. Section 20.4's phrase “this document is the clean integrated version” will become accurate on integration; it should not be read as evidence that the target has already been updated.

There is some repetition of the warnings that a label, equation or exact interface does not supply its intended meaning. Most instances attach to different failures and are useful. The best improvement would be replacing one of the instructional warnings with the positive construction in P1, not appending another warning or broad new chapter.

The remaining universal questions—discovering every relevant distinction, validating all domain interpretations, supplying all realizing powers, proving global adequacy, ensuring termination—are legitimately open. Their nonresolution does not prevent completion of this conceptual definition. Conversely, finite-document completion should not be declared solely because those universal questions have been scoped away. The bounded semantically rich positive construction remains within the present explanatory responsibility.

After P1 and the modest P2 repair, a finite completion judgment would be defensible on substantive grounds: the document would have an actual semantic trace, an actual productive interpretation chain, substantial mathematical constitution and a positive conditional account of operability. Integration into the clean target and a final audit of dependent claims would still be required. Reviewer agreement alone would not supply that evidence.

## Bounded closure review — 9 October 2026

The findings above preserve the earlier candidate cut. I subsequently read the actual additions in §§2.3, 3.4 and 4.3, together with the revised §15.1. This is a closure check of P1 and P2 only, not a second whole-document audit or a completion claim about the clean target.

**P1 is resolved at this definition's explanatory scope.** The new §4.3 subsection, “A complete small connection, rather than a placeholder for one,” gives an actual learner purpose, adopted explanatory achievement, mathematical subject, generated presentation correspondence, explanatory passage, learner activity and assessment subject. Its connecting argument is inspectable: the requested prediction concerns cell count; the two independent position indices determine that count; changing one or both ranges produces the stated one-factor/two-factor distinction. The relationship between representation and subject is constituted through the drawing rule rather than entrusted to an unspecified interpreter of an arbitrary picture.

The changed-purpose counterinterpretation matters. Making existing cells physically larger while retaining the index ranges differs from increasing those ranges. Thus the account shows which distinction a plausible use of “enlargement” can conceal and what a reusable constitution must retain. It also shows positive generative use over admitted positive integers, rather than merely rejecting a topic match. Correctly answering a count is kept separate from understanding the relationship or achieving a pedagogical effect.

Changing from hurricane material to this grid illustration is an adequate substantive repair. My finding required an actual connecting construction; it did not require a fabricated account of hurricane science or a universal content interpreter. Keller's hurricane case remains a source-grounded diagnostic contrast, and the grid fragment supplies the positive bounded construction. The mathematics in this fragment is elementary, but §14 remains the distinct, substantial algebraic investigation; the fragment's contribution is to connect purpose, interpretation, presentation and constructive use.

The revised §15.1 carries the distinction downstream: a generated presentation can inherit its interpreted relationship through construction, whereas understanding unfamiliar content remains an additional contribution. Human interpretation is not declared solved or autonomous. Its continuing limits are consequently a legitimate domain-specific residual, not the original placeholder substituting for the example's central constructive work.

**P2 is resolved.** Section 2.3 now gives an illustrative correction cycle in which a novice's criterion changes from topic association to explanatory role through a contrastive example and criticism. Subsequent accepted uses can stabilize that distinction without encoding a complete recognition procedure. The illustrative status and continued possibility of dispute avoid unsupported empirical generalization. Section 3.4 specifies the relation transferred by the human-compiler analogy and the unsupported assumptions that cannot be inherited from software compilation. The analogy now develops a functional responsibility and its limit rather than merely naming a resemblance.

No remaining intellectual omission within these two findings warrants keeping either open. The original provisional non-completion judgment should therefore be read as superseded for P1/P2. The principal agent still owns integration, its full final audit and the overall goal-completion decision.
