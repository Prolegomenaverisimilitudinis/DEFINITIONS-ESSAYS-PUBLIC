# TechneBase — Working Definition

**Status:** Initial document for developing the definition. No substantive definition has been adopted yet.

## Purpose

Develop a coherent, explanatory account of what TechneBase is, its purpose, its constituents and their relationships. Establish its relationship to Abstography and other concepts through the forthcoming discussion, rather than assuming that relationship in advance.

## Revision discipline

Develop the account progressively. Distinguish agreed definitions from provisional interpretations and open questions. When understanding changes, revise the document throughout so its definitions, explanations and examples remain consistent. Preserve useful material in its appropriate context rather than accumulating contradictory versions.
